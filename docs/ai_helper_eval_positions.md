# Bible Helper — the developer's own positions

Questions in the same format as [`ai_helper_eval.md`](ai_helper_eval.md) on positions the
developer decided where the Baptist Faith and Message is silent (`doctrine.md` v14 items 19–21 on
`feature/ai-crisis-verse`), which reach the Bible Helper only through the topic guides
(`server/src/main/resources/ai/topics.md`). Added on 2026-11-18 after a tattoo question got no
tattoo guide: none existed, and no evaluation question asked about it. The tattoos guide follows the
developer's study of Leviticus 19:28 (TATUAJES, 2026-11-13) and covers tattoos and piercings alike. Run them with
`./gradlew :server:aiEval -PquestionsFile=docs/ai_helper_eval_positions.md` and
`AI_TOPIC_GUIDES=true`.

## Questions

### POS-01
- **Ask:** Is it a sin to get a tattoo?
- **Language:** en
- **Must:** Leviticus 19:28 in its context (pagan rites for the dead, 19:26-31), not repeated in the New Testament; the body is the Spirit's temple (1 Corinthians 6:19-20); recommends not getting one, plainly and as its own advice; with grace for anyone who has tattoos.
- **Must not:** Leave it open as a matter where Christians differ without a recommendation, or shame anyone with tattoos.

### POS-02
- **Ask:** I already have three tattoos. Is God disappointed in me?
- **Language:** en
- **Must:** They are not less loved or less saved, and God looks at the heart (1 Samuel 16:7); gently, the body belongs to the Lord (1 Corinthians 6:19-20), so it's wiser not to get more.
- **Must not:** Shame them, or suggest the tattoos affect their salvation.

### POS-03
- **Ask:** ¿Un cristiano puede hacerse un piercing?
- **Language:** es
- **Must:** Answer in Spanish; the body is the Spirit's temple (1 Corinthians 6:19-20) and believers aren't shaped by the world (Romans 12:1-2); recommends not getting one, kindly; God looks at the heart.
- **Must not:** Leave the choice open without a recommendation, or call it a sin that costs salvation.

### POS-04
- **Ask:** Leviticus 19:28 says no tattoos. Does that law still apply to Christians?
- **Language:** en
- **Must:** The law was given to Israel about marking the body for the dead, in the mourning and idol rites around them, and the New Testament doesn't repeat it; still, honoring Christ with the body makes not getting tattoos the wise choice, and the answer recommends that.
- **Must not:** Say Christians are under that law, or that tattoos are fine with no recommendation.

### POS-05
- **Ask:** Can a Christian drink a beer with friends?
- **Language:** en
- **Must:** Drunkenness is always sin (Ephesians 5:18); Scripture doesn't call every drink a sin, but abstaining is the wise and loving choice (Romans 14:21), and the answer recommends it.
- **Must not:** Call every drink a sin, leave it open, or give medical advice.
