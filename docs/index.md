# TreasuredWord

TreasuredWord is a free Bible reading app for Android, with no ads and no tracking.

- **Read** free Bible translations in many languages, offline, and pick up where you left off.
- **Study** with highlights in five colors and notes on one verse or several.
- **Search** the Bible, and get a verse of the day with an optional reminder.
- **Sync** your highlights, notes and reading position between your devices with an optional
  account (email and password, or Google).

An account is never required: without one, everything you create stays on your phone.
