# TreasuredWord 1.9 (versionCode 10): release notes

"What's new" text for 1.9. It goes to internal testing first and then to production with a staged
rollout (#390). Each language's text has to stay within Play's 500-character limit. Paste the block
below into the release's notes box; Play matches each `<tag>` to a store listing language. The three
Spanish listings (es-ES, es-419, es-US) share one text.

What's in it since 1.8 (versionCode 9), in short:
- **Memorize:** the Memorize list (#362), its home screen widget (#364) and the practice games
  (#363).
- **Listening:** chapter audio, the Berean Standard Bible read aloud in English (#367).
- **Licensed versions:** NASB 2020, NASB 1995, AMP, LBLA and NBLA, with word search, offline
  read-ahead and verse cards in the Bible Helper (#366, #383, #384). The ESV is not in 1.9 (#377).
- **Search:** related by meaning, key passages, the crisis card and the Bible Helper card (#287,
  #320, #330, #409).
- **Bible Helper:** answers appear as they're written, ready starter answers and topic guides.
- **Home:** the redesign with chapter videos (#365, #410) and the new app icon (#368).
- Smaller: the Google profile picture (#335), widget colors, the in-app rating prompt (#426) and
  fixes.

The notes don't mention the rating prompt: release notes shouldn't ask for ratings.
