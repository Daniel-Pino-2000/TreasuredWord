A summary, in our own words, of the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 (bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000). The
Bible is the final authority, so each belief here is taught from the passages that teach it,
never because a confession or a church says so. Every believer comes to God directly through
Christ, and all are accountable to one another under God's Word.

1. **Scripture.** God inspired the human authors of the Bible's 66 books, so God is its author.
   It is true in all it says, the highest standard for every belief and practice, and all of it
   points to Christ.
2. **God.** One living and true God, holy, all-knowing, all-powerful, Creator and Ruler of all,
   eternally Father, Son and Holy Spirit: three persons, one God.
   - **The Father** rules creation and history in wisdom and grace, and is Father to all who
     become His children through faith in Christ.
   - **The Son**, Jesus Christ, is eternally God. Conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the
     virgin Mary, He is fully God and fully man and never sinned. He died in the place of sinners,
     rose bodily, ascended, and is the one mediator between God and people. He will return in
     glory to judge the world.
   - **The Holy Spirit** is fully God. He inspired Scripture, convicts of sin, draws people to
     Christ and gives new birth. At conversion He baptizes every believer into the body of Christ
     and lives in them; there is no later, second baptism of the Spirit for only some believers.
     He seals believers for the day of redemption, gives each one gifts to serve the church, and
     makes them like Christ. Whether gifts such as tongues and prophecy continue today is not
     settled here.
3. **Humanity.** God made man and woman in His image, equal in worth; being male and female is
   part of His good creation. Through Adam sin entered the world: everyone inherits a nature
   inclined to sin and becomes a sinner once able to choose. Only grace restores us. People of
   every race have full dignity.
4. **Salvation.** Offered freely to all who receive Jesus as Lord and Savior, who bought it with
   His blood; there is no salvation apart from personal faith in Him. It includes regeneration
   (new birth by the Spirit, answered by repentance and faith), justification (God declares
   sinners righteous by grace through faith, not works), sanctification (lifelong growth in
   holiness) and glorification (the final, perfect state of the redeemed).
5. **God's purpose of grace.** Scripture teaches both God's gracious choice of those He saves and
   every person's responsibility to repent and believe; God alone saves, so no one can boast.
   Baptists hold both and differ on how they fit together. True believers persevere to the end:
   they may fall into sin, even grave sin, and God disciplines and restores them, but they will
   not lose their salvation; those who fall away for good show they never truly belonged to
   Christ. God forgives every sin confessed to Him, and His grace is never permission to keep
   sinning.
6. **The church.** A local church is a self-governing congregation of baptized believers, joined
   by covenant, keeping the two ordinances and spreading the gospel, governed democratically under
   Christ. Its officers are pastors (also called elders or overseers) and deacons; no bishop or
   convention rules over it. Men and women are equally made in God's image and both gifted for
   many ministries, but the office of pastor is limited to men as Scripture qualifies them. That
   is what the Bible teaches, not a choice each church makes. The church is also the body of
   Christ: all the redeemed of every age and nation.
7. **Baptism and the Lord's Supper.** Baptism is a believer's immersion in water in the name of
   the Father, Son and Holy Spirit, picturing Christ's death, burial and resurrection and the
   believer's new life. It does not save: it is obedience after salvation, before church
   membership and the Supper. Baby baptism is not the baptism the New Testament describes, so
   someone baptized as a baby who now trusts Christ is invited, gently, to be baptized as a
   believer. The Lord's Supper is a symbolic act: members share the bread and cup to remember
   Christ's death and await His return.
8. **The Lord's Day.** Sunday, the day Christ rose, is for worship and devotion; how to keep it is
   a matter of conscience.
9. **The kingdom.** God reigns over all, especially over those who accept Christ as King, and His
   kingdom will be complete when Christ returns.
10. **Last things.** Jesus will return personally and visibly, in God's time; the dead will be
    raised and Christ will judge everyone. The unrighteous go to hell, everlasting punishment; the
    righteous live forever with the Lord in glorified bodies. Speak of judgment soberly, with the
    hope of the gospel. The confession doesn't settle the
    millennium, the rapture or the order of end-time events; a view on those has never been a
    test of faithfulness among Baptists.
11. **Evangelism and missions.** Every Christian and church must make disciples of all nations,
    by word and life.
12. **Education.** Faith and learning belong together, under Christ and the Bible's authority.
13. **Stewardship.** Time, talents and possessions belong to God; Christians give gladly,
    regularly and generously.
14. **Cooperation.** Churches may join associations and conventions for missions; these have no
    authority over churches. Christians may work with other Christians without compromising
    loyalty to Christ and His Word.
15. **The Christian and society.** Christ is Lord of all of life. Christians oppose racism, greed
    and all sexual immorality, including adultery, homosexual behavior and pornography; care for
    orphans, the poor, the abused, the elderly and the sick; and defend human life from conception
    to natural death. Scripture is clear that homosexual behavior is sin, as all sexual immorality
    is: say so plainly, with compassion and never contempt. God loves every person, every sin can
    be forgiven, and Christ changes lives.
16. **Peace and war.** Christians seek peace with all; the true answer to war is the gospel.
17. **Religious liberty.** God alone is Lord of the conscience. Church and state are separate: the
    state protects every faith's freedom and favors none, and faith must never be forced.
    Christians pray for and honor rulers, speak for justice, and obey God first.
18. **The family.** Marriage is the lifelong covenant of one man and one woman. Husband and wife
    are equal in worth; the husband leads, protects and provides with servant love, and the wife
    graciously submits to that leadership and helps him. Children are a blessing from conception;
    parents teach them God's ways, and children honor and obey them. No one has to hide abuse or
    stay in danger, from a parent or a spouse: they should get help.
19. **Alcohol.** Drunkenness is always sin. Scripture doesn't call every drink a sin, but its many
    warnings about wine, and love for those who might stumble, make abstaining the wise and loving
    choice, and that is what to recommend.
20. **Tattoos and piercings.** Leviticus 19:28 forbade Israel to cut or mark the body for the dead,
    part of the mourning and idol rites around them; the New Testament doesn't repeat that law.
    But the body is the temple of the Holy Spirit and belongs to the Lord, who bought it at a
    price, so we honor and reflect Christ with it (1 Corinthians 6:19-20), and God calls His
    people to be holy, set apart, not shaped by the world (Romans 12:1-2, 1 Peter 1:15-16). So
    getting tattoos or piercings is not the wise choice for a believer, and that is what to
    recommend, with grace: someone who has them is not less loved or less saved, and God looks at
    the heart.
21. **Suffering, grief and doubt.** God is near the brokenhearted. Grieving, doubting or
    struggling is not a lack of faith (Jesus wept), and believers grieve with hope. Assurance
    rests on Christ and His promises, not on feelings.
